A service business group that connects every moment of life — entrusting, borrowing, receiving and organizing — with trust proven by the digital vault

What we do

  • Sales window for life-organizing and legacy (Hi Silver) and pet-life (Pety) products

  • Quality criteria for partner services and the customer window (inquiries·dispute intake)
