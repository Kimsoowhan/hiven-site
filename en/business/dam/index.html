Hi DAM

Digital asset classification standards and original-uniqueness verification

Every business starts with Hi Coffer, a digital vault for individuals.See Hi Coffer

About this group

A business group that establishes, publishes and distributes standard classification systems for documents and digital assets (data·file·folder·code), verifies the uniqueness of originals (hash·ledger), and supplies these systems to every business group and customer as GS-certified products (Hi DAM engine + HiAad)

Current stage:Stage 1 B2C — the Hi DAM engine (free) and HiAad (free offline rule packs), and publication of 12 classification standard documents. Released together with the Hi Coffer PC beta.

What we do

  • Establishing, publishing and revising 12 classification standards
  • Developing and supplying engines for identification (ASSET_ID), distinction, confirmation, selection, ledger and restoration
  • Developing HiAad and producing and publishing rule packs
  • Documents and testing for GS certification and standard registration
  • Criteria for consistency checks of the relationship network (nodes·links) and first-edition supply

Business lines

  • Stage 1 B2C — Hi DAM engine (free) + HiAad (free offline rule packs) · Publication of 12 classification standard documents · Released together with the Hi Coffer PC beta
  • Stage 2 B2B — Central rule-pack distribution and enterprise subscriptions (per device) · Managed services · Key hierarchy (managers·departments)
  • Stage 3 B2G — Institutional rule packs and evidence logs · Mapping tables for BRM and records-management schedules · Proposals for association standards and KS
