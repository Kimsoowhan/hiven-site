About this group

HIVEN's eighth business group. By adding monitoring, AX and UX to user and entity behavior analytics (UEBA), it helps companies and institutions detect and respond early to insider threats, account takeover and privilege misuse. It is the direction in which Hi DAM, Hi Coffer and Hi Silver expand.

Current stage:In preparation

What we do

  • Connects three layers — collection → behavior analytics → monitoring and response — on top of the security systems already in use
  • Analysis that uses the usual behavior of the same job group as a baseline, and risk scores that show why something is risky
  • Four-stage autonomous monitoring: alert triage → in-depth investigation → response and isolation → policy optimization
  • A lightweight, security-specialized language model kept inside the customer so data does not leave, with safeguards that require approval for important actions

Business lines

  • Finance and fintech
  • Advanced manufacturing and IT
  • Public sector and local governments
