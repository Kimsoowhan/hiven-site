2. Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-24 · Applies to: Hi Coffer AI Agent, distributed and downloaded as a beta (test) version

In accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act, the operator of the Hi Coffer AI Agent beta protects users' personal information and sets out this Privacy Policy so that related complaints can be handled promptly.

1. Controller

The current controller is the representative (founder of HIVEN, in incorporation).
After HIVEN is incorporated, control transfers to HIVEN with prior notice on this page and in the app.

2. Processing in the app

There is no sign-up.
All processing (file organizing, vault) happens only on the user's PC and nothing is sent to the internet automatically.
